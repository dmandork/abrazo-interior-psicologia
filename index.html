Hello, I'm Ivy Monje

I am a psychologist by profession from Universidad Católica Boliviana San Pablo with a humanistic cognitive-behavioral approach. I specialize in organizational psychology, human resources, and educational psychology. I accompany individuals and institutions in processes of healing, growth, and inner transformation.

My practice is based on deep respect for each person's unique story and the conviction that an inner embrace is the first step to heal and flourish.
